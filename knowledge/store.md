# Shopping at ElitePC

This guide describes the current shopping features. For a product's current price, availability, stock, and specifications, check the live catalog. General hardware guidance is not a substitute for the details of the exact product you are considering.

## Browsing and Searching the Catalog

You can browse products and categories without signing in. The catalog page lets you search by product name and filter the displayed products by category. Clear the search or select All to broaden the displayed results.

Open a product to view its description, image, current displayed price and stock status, and the specifications available for that listing. Some products have more detailed specifications than others. Missing information should not be treated as a confirmed feature.

The storefront displays monetary amounts in USD. Consult the current listing rather than relying on a price mentioned in an earlier conversation.

## Creating and Using an Account

Create a customer account with your name, email address, and password. Sign in with your email and password, and use Sign Out when finished.

An account is required to place an order and view your order history or invoices. Browsing products, using a guest cart or favorites list, and asking Elite AI shopping questions do not require an account.

Signing in again can end a previous sign-in session. If a session is no longer valid, sign in again to access account-specific shopping information.

## Guest and Account Carts

Use Add to Cart on a product, then open the cart to review items, increase or decrease quantities, or remove a line. The cart shows unit prices, quantities, subtotals, and an estimated total. Products shown as out of stock cannot be added through their product-page button.

When signed out, your cart is saved in the current browser. Clearing that browser's stored site data can remove it. When signed in, you use the cart saved to your account.

Guest and account carts are separate. Signing in does not automatically merge guest items into your account cart; signing out returns you to the guest cart saved in that browser. Review the account cart before continuing to checkout.

Adding an item to a cart does not reserve stock. Requested quantities are checked for the signed-in cart, and availability is checked again when an order is placed.

## Saving and Removing Favorites

Use a product's heart control to save it for later or remove it from favorites. The Favorites page shows your saved selection; saving a product is separate from adding it to your cart.

Guest favorites belong to the current browser. Signed-in favorites belong to the account. Signing in does not automatically import the guest list, and signing out returns to the browser's guest favorites.

A favorite is not a reservation, an order, or a promise that the product will remain available. Consult the current listing when you are ready to buy.

## Reviewing and Placing an Order

Sign in, review your account cart, and proceed to Checkout. Enter a delivery address and contact phone number, choose one of the listed payment-method options, and review the order summary before selecting Place Order.

Prices and available quantities are checked when the order is created. A stock shortage or invalid form entry can prevent order creation and display an error. Review that message and the cart before submitting again.

A successful submission displays an order number and confirmed total, with a link to My Orders. Purchased cart lines are removed when they still match what was submitted; other or subsequently changed items can remain.

An error or missing confirmation is not proof of a completed purchase. Check My Orders before submitting again if you are unsure whether the order was created.

## Payment-Method Selection

Checkout currently lists Credit card, PayPal, and Cash on delivery as choices. The checkout page does not request card details.

Selecting one of these options records the chosen payment method with the order. An order-created confirmation is not, by itself, confirmation that a card charge or PayPal payment has completed. Do not provide payment credentials to Elite AI.

## Viewing Orders and Invoices

After signing in, open My Orders to see your orders, placement dates, displayed statuses, and totals. Open View Details to see purchased items, quantities, recorded unit prices, delivery information, and the selected payment method.

The displayed order statuses include In progress, Confirmed, and Cancelled. A status label describes the order record; it is not live carrier tracking. An order may also display an estimated delivery date, which is an estimate rather than a delivery guarantee.

Use Download Invoice on an order's details page to download its PDF invoice. Customer order details and invoices are available to the account that placed the order. The customer order pages do not offer controls to cancel, edit, or refund an order.

## Using Elite AI

Elite AI is a read-only shopping assistant available without signing in. It can help search the current catalog, explain product details, list categories, check stock, compare products, and discuss compatibility evidence.

For useful guidance, describe your games or applications, monitor resolution, budget, and any components you already own. Elite AI may ask for clarification when product names or requirements are ambiguous.

Elite AI cannot change your cart or favorites, place or cancel orders, process payments or refunds, modify your account, or access private order information. Use the shopping pages to make supported changes yourself.

When required compatibility specifications are missing, Elite AI must explain that there is insufficient verified information rather than claim that the parts work together. Chat history is kept only for the current page session and resets when your account or sign-in session changes.
